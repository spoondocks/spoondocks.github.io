Vault Sync Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026.

Vault Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner, Conner Spooner, on his own computer. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's own consent, Vault Sync reads and organizes the owner's Google Calendar and Gmail (reading messages, applying labels, archiving, and creating drafts) so they can be shown in his private notes and dashboards.

Where the data goes

Data stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and it is not used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security page under third-party connections.
